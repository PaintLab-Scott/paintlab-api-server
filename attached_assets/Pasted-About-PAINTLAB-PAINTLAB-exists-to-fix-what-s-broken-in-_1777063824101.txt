About PAINTLAB

PAINTLAB exists to fix what’s broken in commercial painting.

The industry is built on inconsistency—unpredictable pricing, unreliable timelines, uneven quality, and constant rebidding. That doesn’t work for operators managing real assets, real budgets, and real expectations.

We built PAINTLAB to be different.

We engineer paint and coating systems—not one-off projects. That means every scope is designed for consistency, durability, and repeatable execution across your portfolio.

We operate as a long-term partner, not a transactional vendor. The result is predictable costs, reliable timelines, clear communication, and outcomes you can trust—every time.

Our approach is systems-driven: clearly defined scopes, repeatable processes, disciplined crews, and accountability at every step. The result is high-grade craftsmanship delivered with the professionalism and reliability that commercial properties actually require.

This isn’t about painting walls.
It’s about creating a dependable, scalable way to maintain and elevate your asset—without the friction.

Leadership

PAINTLAB was founded by Scott Gendelman, a commercial operator with over 20 years of experience across technology, business development, operations, and marketing.

Having lived in Austin for over 14 years, Scott understands firsthand how competitive and fast-moving the commercial and multifamily real estate market is—and the level of execution it demands. Property managers, asset owners, and facilities leaders don’t have room for inconsistency.

Throughout his career, he saw the same challenges repeat: unreliable vendors, uneven quality, unclear pricing, and constant friction in execution.

PAINTLAB was built to solve that.

By applying a systems-driven, operator-first mindset, Scott set out to redefine how paint and coating systems are delivered—bringing predictability, accountability, and long-term partnership to an industry that has historically lacked all three.

The goal is simple: deliver a better, more dependable way to maintain and elevate commercial assets.
